# Ayam Mbak Rina — Landing Page UI (FigCraft) — Design

## Intent
A landing page that lets buyers see the menu and prices, then chat the nearest branch on WhatsApp.

## Approaches
- **FigCraft design first (chosen):** iterate visually before coding.
- HTML/Next.js site directly: deferred until the design is approved.

## Deliverable
One FigCraft page with two frames: Desktop (1440px) and Mobile (390px).

## Visual
- Colors: red `#C8102E` (accents, buttons), yellow `#FFC72C` (highlights, prices), cream `#FFF8EC` (background), dark text `#2A1A12`.
- Type: bold display font for headings (Bricolage Grotesque or Poppins Black), Inter for body.
- Photos from `assets/` in rounded frames on cream so uneven WhatsApp photo quality reads as consistent.
- Text logo "Ayam Mbak Rina" until a real logo file exists (our call).

## Sections
1. Nav: text logo, Menu, Lokasi, "Pesan WA" button.
2. Hero: "Ayam Bumbu Kuning khas pasar" with a boxed chicken photo and two WA buttons, one per branch.
3. Yellow strip: free sambal (bawang/terasi), "harga bisa menyesuaikan".
4. Menu: card grid with name and price. The 4 signature items get photos.
   - Ayam bumbu kuning 17k, ayam rempah laos 17k, ayam bumbu klaten 17k, ayam bumbu rujak pedas 20k
   - Bebek bumbu kuning 30k, kepala ayam/bebek 5k, ati ampela ayam/bebek 5k
   - Ayam utuh ekor 75–80k, bebek utuh ekor 100k, nasi box from 30k
5. Nasi Box & whole birds: basket photo, "start 30k", invitation to order for events.
6. Our kitchen: 3 spice/sambal photos.
7. Locations: Pasar Gang Baru no. 78 (082229302678) and Pasar Prembaen no. 1046 (081225557578), each with a WA button.
8. Red footer.

## Non-goals
Code, online ordering, additional pages.
